How we verify DCR

DCR is an independent solver built to be correct, then confirmed against known references. This page shows the methodology and representative cross-verification results — the engineering facts are open; the presentation is our own.

Within ±3% of industry references KDS · KBC · KCI · FEMA OpenSees (UC Berkeley)
Methodology

Four ways we keep results honest

🧮

OpenSees foundation

Built on the UC Berkeley OpenSees finite-element solver — a world-standard engine — rather than an opaque in-house black box.

📐

Converged to theory

Results are mesh-converged against analytical and code-theoretical solutions — DCR aims to be correct, not tuned to match any one competitor.

🔗

Standards-traceable

Every design value traces back to its governing combination and KDS/KCI/FEMA clause through Result DNA.

⚖️

Independent cross-checks

Modal periods, member forces and design ratios are cross-verified against established reference models (MIDAS / ETABS).
