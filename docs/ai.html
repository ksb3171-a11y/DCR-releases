Privacy & control

Your key. Your data. Your machine.

AI is opt-in and transparent — you bring the key, you see the cost, and your project is never uploaded to us.

Bring your own key

Use your own Anthropic API key. You control access and spend — no STRIX markup, no lock-in.

Local-first

The AI reads your model on your machine and talks directly to the provider. Your project files are never sent to STRIX servers.

✅

AI features stay off until you add a key. Nothing runs, and nothing is sent, unless you turn it on.
