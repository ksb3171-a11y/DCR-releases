How licensing works

Account first, then licenses

One account holds concurrent-use licenses. Individuals subscribe to 1 license; companies subscribe to the licenses they need and invite members. The number of licenses you hold sets how many people can run STRIX at the same time.

🏢AccountBilling account for a person or company
→
🎟️Licenses heldConcurrent users = subscriptions bought
→
👥MembersYour employees' own logins, sharing your licenses
🔄

Concurrent-use licenses

Licenses are shared, not assigned. When a colleague signs out, the license frees up for the next person — no per-person management.

👥

Employees as members

Add your employees as named members at no extra cost. Members are limited to the account owner's employees. You only pay for how many work at the same time.

🧾

One bill per account

Manage payments and receipts from one account. The account owner manages licenses and members.
