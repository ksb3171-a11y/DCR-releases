How licensing works

Company first, then seats

One company account holds a pool of floating seats. Anyone on your team can have an account; your seat count caps how many run STRIX at the same time.

🏢CompanyTop-level account & billing owner
→
🎟️Seat poolConcurrent users = subscriptions bought
→
👥MembersUnlimited named logins, share the pool
🔄

Floating concurrency

Seats are shared, not assigned. When a colleague signs out, the seat frees up for the next person — no per-person license juggling.

∞

Unlimited members

Add your whole team as named members at no extra cost. You only pay for how many work at the same time.

🧾

One company bill

Centralized billing, receipts and Korean tax invoices under the company account. The owner manages seats and members.
